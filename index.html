HOW TO GET & ACTIVATE A LICENSE

JARVIS Pro is in manual early access; online checkout is not enabled. To request a key, contact the JARVIS publisher through the support channel provided with your installer. The publisher issues it privately—never post or share your key publicly.

  1. Enter your issued key in the validator above to check that it is active. This website check does not consume a device activation.
  2. Install JARVIS Pro, paste the same key into the activation screen, and activate your device while online.
  3. Keep the key for future reinstalls; device limits, expiry, or revocation are managed by the publisher.

SAFE LICENSE ENFORCEMENT

If a key expires or is revoked, Pro activation is blocked and the app asks the operator to renew or sign out. It does not silently uninstall software or delete user data.
